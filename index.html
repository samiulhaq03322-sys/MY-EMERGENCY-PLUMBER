Our Services

Comprehensive emergency and general plumbing for Greater Manchester homes and businesses.

Blocked Drains & Toilets

Obstructions in your drainage system can lead to serious health hazards and property damage. We use the latest high-pressure water jetting technology and CCTV surveys to identify and clear blockages fast.

  • High-pressure drain jetting
  • CCTV drain inspections
  • Toilet and sink unblocking
  • Root removal

Burst Pipes & Leaks

A burst pipe can flood your home in minutes. Our emergency plumbers are experts in tracing leaks through walls and under floors using acoustic equipment and thermal imaging, minimizing disruption to your home.

  • Emergency pipe repairs
  • Leak detection
  • Tap repairs and replacement
  • Pipe lagging and winter protection
